\section{Gating}
A phase does not begin until the prior phase's \textbf{docs} are written and its tests (where
applicable) pass. If time runs short, cut in this order: STRETCH $\rightarrow$ Completer $\rightarrow$
Consolidator (degrades to a summary stub) $\rightarrow$ Prospective Queue $\rightarrow$ Ranker + Compressor.
\textbf{Never cut the Surprise Gate, the eval harness, or the demo video.}

\section{Mandates Honoured During the Build}
Document First $\rightarrow$ Implement $\rightarrow$ Test $\rightarrow$ Conclude; atomic commits
after every step; every \texttt{.tex} auto-compiled to PDF; env vars in both \texttt{orch.config.json}
and \texttt{.env.example}.
