\chapter{Plan Audit (Numbered Outline and Method)}

This chapter makes the plan \emph{auditable}. Every part of it carries a stable ID so a second
reviewer (human or agent) can judge it section by section without asking for context.

\section{Audit Method}
For each numbered item we record one verdict against six fixed axes:
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.16\textwidth}p{0.76\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Axis & Question \\
\midrule
Feasibility & Can one person build this Oct 27--Nov 4 alongside work and study? \\
Dependency & What must exist first? Does it already exist (Reuse Map)? \\
Risk & What breaks it --- live on stage, or under judge scrutiny? \\
Rubric & Which judging column it serves (Impact / Meta tooling / Technical depth / Demo). \\
Cost & Effort: S (hours) $\cdot$ M (a day) $\cdot$ L (multi-day). \\
Verdict & Keep $\cdot$ Improve $\cdot$ Cut $\cdot$ Add $\cdot$ Defer. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Independent-Review Questions (for third-party judges)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Does it satisfy the problem statement (\emph{Build for Your World} / \emph{Push the Frontier})?
  \item Is Meta's tooling central, not decorative?
  \item Is the engineering sound and thought through?
  \item Is the demo compelling \emph{and live-safe}?
  \item Is it clearly new work, distinguishable from reuse?
  \item Is anything here a banned anti-project?
  \item Are the claims measurable (not vibes)?
  \item Is the scope honest for 9 solo days?
\end{enumerate}
