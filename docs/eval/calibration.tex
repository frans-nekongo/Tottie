\chapter{Calibration Ledger: Every Knob, Measured or Guessed}

Every threshold, cap, weight, and TTL in the system, with its provenance.
\textit{Measured} means a number was observed and the value chosen from the
observation; \textit{literature} means a published default; \textit{guess}
means a placeholder its author picked and the harness must one day earn.
New knobs land here as guesses first. No silent magic numbers.

\section{Encode path (Observer + Scribe)}

\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{TOTTIE\_FAMILIARITY\_COSINE = 0.80} --- \textbf{measured}.
Live calibration: exact repeat 1.00, paraphrase 0.88, topical-novel 0.68,
unrelated 0.32. Floor sits in the 0.68--0.88 gap. Replaces the dead
session-centroid surprise gate, which dropped a 0.68-novel turn at 0.25.
\item Observer \texttt{max\_tokens = 2048} + \texttt{reasoning\_effort =
minimal} --- \textbf{measured}. Default effort burned 2046/2048 tokens
thinking and returned nothing (6/6 live turns RAW); minimal lands in
\textasciitilde325 reasoning tokens with content. Clerk tasks never pay
thinker budgets.
\item \texttt{TOTTIE\_RECONCILE\_COSINE = 0.86} --- \textbf{measured}
(harness-calibrated per config; see eval reports).
\item \texttt{TOTTIE\_ACTIVE\_COSINE = 0.60} --- \textbf{measured} (10-pair
calibration; labile route).
\item Kind fallback \texttt{event} and tier fallback \texttt{episodic} ---
\textbf{guess} (safe defaults for empty model output only).
\item Tag cap 12 per candidate --- \textbf{guess}, documented DOS guard
(the prompt itself is unbounded: as-many-as-fit).
\item \texttt{TOTTIE\_IDENTITY\_SALIENCE} boost --- \textbf{guess}.
\item Familiar re-mention utility +0.05 --- \textbf{guess}, ordered below
retrieval practice by the testing effect.
\end{itemize}

\section{How a guess graduates}

Reproduce the Batch-1 method: measure the quantity live (both sides of the
boundary), pick the value from the gap, pin it with a deterministic test,
move the row to \textit{measured} with the evidence cited. The harness
(\texttt{eval/harness.py}) is the standing instrument; one-off
calibrations name their script or session in the commit message.
